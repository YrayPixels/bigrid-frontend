# 2. Who we're targeting

I wouldn't target "all Nigerian businesses."

I'd start with businesses that are **already selling but haven't properly digitized**.

### Primary audience

* Fashion businesses
* Beauty businesses
* Food businesses
* Electronics
* Home & lifestyle
* Accessories
* Cosmetics
* SMEs selling through WhatsApp
* Instagram businesses
* Facebook sellers
* Small retailers
* Service businesses

The ideal person is:

> **"I already sell something. I just don't have a proper online store."**

That's your sweet spot.

Someone who hasn't started a business yet is much less valuable to Bizgrid.

# 3. Eligibility

I'd make participation **free**.

But there should be requirements.

### To become an official BizFest participant:

**Step 1**

Create Bizgrid account.

**Step 2**

Create store.

**Step 3**

Add at least 5 products/services.

**Step 4**

Complete business profile.

**Step 5**

Publish store.

**Step 6**

Receive at least one successful transaction.

That final requirement is extremely important.

Because:

> **Registration ≠ activation.**

# 4. The BizFest Score

This becomes the backbone of the competition.

I'd initially use something like:

| Activity                  | Points |
| ------------------------- | -----: |
| Create Bizgrid store      |     10 |
| Complete business profile |      5 |
| Add 5 products            |      5 |
| Publish store             |     10 |
| First transaction         |     20 |
| 5 transactions            |     10 |
| 10 transactions           |     10 |
| Customer reviews          |      5 |
| Attend workshops          |     10 |
| Complete challenges       |      5 |
| Refer businesses          |      5 |
| **Total**                 | **95** |

We can refine the weighting later.

But I would **not make raw sales revenue the entire scoring system.**

Otherwise a business doing ₦20M/month automatically destroys a business doing ₦200K/month.

Instead, reward **growth and execution**.

# 5. Make growth the competition

> **"Who demonstrated the strongest business growth?"**

### Business A

₦500K → ₦1.2M

**140% growth**

### Business B

₦10M → ₦11M

**10% growth**

Business A should have a serious chance of beating Business B.

This makes the competition accessible to smaller businesses.
